# ABPIV Creative Production Adapter

Use this pack when Creative Production needs ABPIV-branded visuals, copy, prompts, social animation frames, web modules, decks, ads, or mood boards.

## Start Here

1. Read `README.md` for the full brand system.
2. Read `prompt-contract.md` before generating prompts or artifacts.
3. Use `creative-production.manifest.json` as the machine-readable map of assets, tokens, surfaces, copy guardrails, and Creative Production path prompts.
4. Use `style-intake.json` when a compact style picker is useful before generation.

## Durable Output Rule

Generated Creative Production work that uses this brand should be saved under:

`outputs/creative-production/abpiv/<brief-slug>/`

Keep generated images, prompts, manifests, review files, and handoff notes together in that folder.

## Brand Frame

ABPIV is the personal brand for Allan B. Pedin IV: founder, researcher, operator. The brand posture is a capital-worthy founder thesis rendered as a quiet technical executive interface.

Primary line:

`Expanding individual agency through emerging technologies that help people and systems coordinate.`

Thesis:

`Build, explain, earn trust.`

Use only the proof signals already in the pack:

- Published AI & Blockchain Researcher
- CipherPlay / RANDAO founder
- Web3 Thought Leader

Do not invent traction, revenue, customers, partners, investors, awards, or metrics.

## Creative Production Fit

- `Explore` - route a broad ABPIV brief into Positioning, Mood boards, Scenes, Offers, Ads, Shots, Logos, or Assets.
- `Mood boards` - explore premium founder-thesis visual territories.
- `Scenes` - show Allan, the work, or the thesis in credible business, research, investor, or conference contexts.
- `Offers` - test the core ABPIV promise across social, site, deck, and founder-content families.
- `Ads` - generate paid or organic social directions without hype or invented proof.
- `Shots` - explore crops or treatments around the headshot, wordmark, system-map motif, or social frames.
- `Logos` - explore typographic ABPIV wordmark/lockup variations only; do not invent a pictorial mascot.
- `Assets` - produce final social frames, animation stills, hero images, slide modules, or launch cards from a selected direction.
