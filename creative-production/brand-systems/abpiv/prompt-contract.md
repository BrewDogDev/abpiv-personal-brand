# ABPIV Prompt Contract

Paste or reference this block before any ABPIV Creative Production prompt.

## Base Context

Create for the ABPIV personal brand: Allan B. Pedin IV, founder, researcher, and operator. Position him as a Visionary Operator CEO: vision plus the discipline to ship. The core positioning is:

`Expanding individual agency through emerging technologies that help people and systems coordinate.`

The operating thesis is:

`Build, explain, earn trust.`

Audience priority: investors first, then partners, media, collaborators, talent, and technical audiences.

Use only these proof signals unless the user supplies new verified source material:

- Published AI & Blockchain Researcher
- CipherPlay / RANDAO founder
- Web3 Thought Leader

## Visual Direction

The visual world is a capital-worthy founder thesis rendered as a quiet technical executive interface.

- Deep near-black navy canvas, not pure black.
- Cyan systems accent for route lines, signal paths, intelligence, coordination, and active motion.
- Sparse amber for human agency, trust, and one earned emphasis per frame.
- High-contrast light text, cool hairline borders, restrained elevation.
- Schibsted Grotesk for display and body.
- IBM Plex Mono only for proof chips, metadata, indices, and protocol details.
- Full-color headshot, framed with a hairline border and subtle navy scrim only when needed.
- System-map motif: thin cyan routes, alignment grids, and signal traces at very low opacity.
- Motion should align, resolve, reveal, and draw routes. Use one soft amber pulse at the focal moment.

## Copy Rules

- Voice is visionary but grounded: confident, never hype.
- Use short declarative sentences.
- Prefer third person for brand framing.
- Avoid `I`.
- Do not invent traction, customers, partners, investors, revenue, metrics, or awards.
- No emoji.
- No exclamation marks.
- No generic AI glow, particle fields, purple gradients, crypto-influencer energy, or buzzword stacking.
